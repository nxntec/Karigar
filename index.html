How Karigar stays free for homeowners

The platform earns from karigars getting work — not from families needing repairs.

This mirrors how Angi and UrbanCompany fund themselves, tuned to what a small local karigar can actually afford to pay for a steady stream of nearby jobs.

Per-lead fee

₹19–₹79 per job lead

Karigars pay a small fee only when matched to a real, nearby job request — priced by job value, not a flat rate that hurts small operators.

Verified badge

₹499 one-time onboarding

Covers ID, address and police verification. This is what homeowners are actually paying trust for — so it's priced to just cover the check itself.

Pro subscription

₹299–₹899 / month

Optional plan for full-time karigars: priority matching, more monthly leads, and a profile page they can share on WhatsApp.

AMC referral

10–15% on annual contracts

Small commission when a homeowner signs an AMC (AC/appliance annual maintenance) through the platform — high-margin, low-frequency for us.
